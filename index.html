The arepa.\nReimagined.

A food rooted in Colombian tradition, reimagined for today's lifestyle.

New to arepas?

An arepa is a Colombian staple: a round, golden, griddled cake enjoyed at breakfast, alongside a meal, or filled with your favorites. Marepa keeps that soul and brings it into your modern routine.
